### About the Experiment 
An upsetting press (Horizontal Forging Machine) is a specialized mechanical power press designed to provide a two-stage motion: clamping the workpiece and heading (deforming) the material. Extrusion press theory is based on the High-Pressure Vessel principle and Controlled Displacement.
